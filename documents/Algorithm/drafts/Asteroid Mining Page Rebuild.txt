# Stage 3 — Feasibility-first

This is the core.

The v1/v2 problem was:

```text id="2ep1np"

routing later
```

so everything collapsed.

Must always:

```text id="fb0mps"

```

That is:


