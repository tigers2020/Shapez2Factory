# Shapez Vortex Asset Attribution

This project vendors a small proof-of-concept subset of shape model assets from
[DontMash/shapez-vortex](https://github.com/DontMash/shapez-vortex), specifically
`static/models/shapes` files for `ShapeDefaultR`, `ShapeDefaultC`, `ShapeDefaultS`,
`ShapeDefaultW`, and `ShapeDefaultP`.

The shapez-vortex repository is distributed under the MIT License. Before public
or commercial distribution, verify whether these model files include or derive
from Shapez 2 game assets and whether additional attribution or permission is
required by the game or asset policy.
